The INDUSHI Story

Why Choose Between Indonesian Spice & Sushi Presentation?

Traditional sushi is often cold and raw, contrasting with Indonesian food lovers who crave hot, aromatic, and heavily seasoned meals. Meanwhile, classic nasi boxes can be messy for corporate events or finger-food parties.

INDUSHI bridges both worlds: 100% cooked warm meats, authentic regional sambals, and bite-sized perfection.
